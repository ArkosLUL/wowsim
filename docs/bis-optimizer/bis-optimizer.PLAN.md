# BiS optimizer

## Context

The user wants a Best-in-Slot generator for their server. It covers one character, or every DPS and tank
raider in the roster, for each cumulative phase:

| Phase | Content | Server tier |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Naxx, EoE, OS | 13 |
| 2 | Ulduar | 14 |
| 3 | ToC | 15 |
| 4 | ICC | 16 |
| 5 | RS | 17 |

It picks items, gems, enchants, reforges and racial traits.

Nothing in the repo optimizes gear today ([INVESTIGATION](bis-optimizer.INVESTIGATION.md)). Results follow
the sim, so they're provisional until parity lands. It runs as `BIS-` work items in the
[wave loop](../wave-loop/wave-loop.PLAN.md).

## Decisions (settled with the user)

**Scope**
- The optimizer changes items, gems, enchants, reforges and racial traits.
- Talents, glyphs, professions and base race stay fixed.
- Healers are out of scope. So is an "owned items only" mode, which is a follow-up.
- Every raider gets all 5 phases.

**Objective**
- The individual tab optimizes the player's own metrics against its buffs panel.
- The raid batch optimizes DPS raiders for raid contribution (raid DPS). Tanks keep the survival/threat
  blend in the batch too.
- Raid contribution is hybrid:
  - The search runs in the individual context, with buffs and debuffs derived from the roster plus
    analytic contribution terms (Demonic Pact SP, Heroic Presence party hit).
  - Raid sims with paired random numbers then re-rank the verified top sets, the per-slot alternatives and
    the final pick.
- The batch runs in two stages per phase. Stage 1 gives everyone a Quick own-metrics BiS. Stage 2
  re-optimizes, against the others' stage 1 BiS, only raiders whose gear changes others' damage, and
  never keeps a pick that doesn't beat their stage 1 pick in the raid. Each party gets at most one
  Draenei, chosen by raid sims (the user, 2026-09-27, after wave J's stage 2 lost to stage 1).

**Tanks**
- One slider from survival (TMI) to threat (TPS), default 70% survival. The 6-metric EP-ratio editor sits
  under "Advanced".
- Crit immunity is a hard constraint, on by default. It comes from the sim's own crit-taken formula, not a
  fixed 540 defense.
- The healing model is re-derived for each phase's boss with `Player.setDefaultHealingParams`' defaults.

**Other constraints**
- Optional per-stat floors. DPS specs also get hit and expertise cap floors, and casters and hunters a
  haste ceiling, on by default ([BIS-caps](#bis-caps-wave-l)).
- Racial traits: screen all 10 races, then fully optimize the top 3 plus ties. "Keep current traits" skips
  the search.

**Encounters**
- DPS: a plain single-target level-83 boss, with duration and variation taken from the current encounter.
- Tanks: each phase's boss with its swing and the damage armor and avoidance can't remove: Kel'Thuzad 25,
  Algalon 25, Anub'arak 25H, the Lich King 25H, Halion 25H
  ([tank-modeling](../guide/tank-modeling.md)).

**Sources and the catalog**
- Sources: all PvE (10 and 25, normal and heroic, heroic 5-mans, emblem vendors, crafted, quest, rep), with
  checkboxes. PvP and mod-dungeon-master's random rewards are out.
- Item availability comes from a server-derived catalog, `assets/database/server_catalog.json`, built first
  ([ADR 0005](../adr/0005-server-item-catalog.md)).
  - Crafted and quest items resolve recursively. A Classic phase is used only as a flagged fallback.
  - A crafted item needs its recipe too: it opens at the later of its reagents' tier and its recipe's. So
    the epic cuts and Nightmare Tear open at 15, with Timothy Jones' designs (quest 66015).
  - Venture Coin (37836) is a PvP currency, so what it buys counts as PvP.
  - It covers every obtainable equippable item and gem, with its stat count, limits and faction.
  - It's regenerated by hand; the tool prints a diff summary.
  - The whole UI switches to catalog tiers: the gear picker and gem EP.
- Tier rules are in [azerothcore-server](../guide/azerothcore-server.md#progression-tiers), including
  Onyxia counting as tier 15.

**UI**
- The tab and the batch are disabled under wasm.
- The batch shows a raiders × phases grid:
  - Each cell: score Δ, plus raid-DPS Δ ± se for DPS raiders. Warnings for a calibration gap over 3% and
    for effects the sim doesn't model.
  - A cell opens the gear (gems, enchants, reforges), the racial traits, per-slot alternatives, and the
    sheet with its caps.
  - Per phase: Validate, Apply, Save gear sets.
  - Export JSON.
  - Default effort is Quick; Normal overnight. Progress survives a reload.
- Saved gear sets are named "<Raider> P<N> BiS" (batch) or "P<N> BiS (optimizer)" (tab).
- Results carry the sim commit and the catalog date.

**Presets**
- New `ui/<spec>/gear_sets/p{1..5}_bis[_<tree>].gear.json` files become the presets and `defaultGear`.
- The Classic presets leave the UI list; files the tests use stay.
- One per phase for each talent tree with gear presets today, on that tree's talents and glyphs, with all
  11 professions. The chosen racial traits go in the tooltip.
- Alliance only, like the user's roster: an Alliance base race and Alliance items (the user, 2026-09-28).

## Architecture

The UI owns candidate pools and settings. Go owns every equip rule and the search.

- The UI's `pool_builder.ts` builds `OptimizeGearRequest{base RaidSimRequest, target_raid_index, settings,
  CandidatePool}`.
- A pool is `player.getItems(slot)`, filtered by:
  - source
  - the catalog: tier ≤ 12+phase, faction, no PvP
  - outclassed gear, except what the target wears ([BIS-outclassed](#bis-outclassed-wave-r-done))
  - `enchantAppliesToItem` and `canEquipEnchant`
- `getMetaGemCondition` becomes linear color constraints.
- The request goes to `/optimizeGearAsync` or `wowsimcli optimize`, then into `sim/optimizer`, which imports
  `sim/core`.

| `sim/optimizer` file | Holds |
|---|---|
| `types.go` | the contract, frozen after wave A |
| `request.go` | calls `core.AddToDatabase` once, then strips every `Player.database` |
| `pool.go` | stat vectors, sockets, reforge options (`core.CanReforge` over the item's `server_stats` and the run's `Reforging`), effect flags |
| `rules.go` | weapon combos, Titan's Grip, dual wield; limits (catalog `maxcount`/`ItemLimitCategory`/unique-equipped, JC ≤ 3); metas (`core.ColorIntersects`); BS and buckle sockets; floors. It never emits states `EquipItem` re-slots |
| `evaluator.go` | the `Evaluator` interface (the test seam): paired `IsTest` RNG with a fixed seed; shards `RandomSeed + k·m`; recover, cancel, cache |
| `objective.go` | J = Σ rₘ·Mₘ/wₘ(ref) over dps, hps, tps, dtps, tmi, pdeath. Normalizers are frozen at the seed; Armor is the reference for dtps, tmi and pdeath. Raid mode: J = raid DPS |
| `response.go` | response curves per stat family. Cap-prone families (hit, spell hit, expertise, ArP, defense) get 5 offsets and a bisected breakpoint |
| `residuals.go` | effect value = paired sim Δ − surrogate Δ, for trinkets, relics, weapons, metas, effect enchants and 2/4-piece macros. About 63 hard-coded ID checks bypass `core.HasItemEffect`, so those slots are always simmed |
| `surrogate.go`, `search.go` | separable surrogate plus residuals; dominance pruning; annealing with restarts (greedy, seed, previous phase) plus coordinate polish; exact per-item gem DP; keeps the top 20 distinct sets |
| `verify.go`, `neighborhood.go` | accepts when Δ̄ > 2·se and Δ̄ > 0.05% J; top 5 per slot, which are also the alternatives |
| `racial.go`, `critimmunity.go` | the racial screen; D* by bisecting bonus defense with `core.ComputePlayerSheet` |
| `sheet.go` | the target's `core.ComputePlayerSheet` in a loadout, memoized per `Loadout` on the `Pool`, which assumes the base never changes after `CompilePool` |
| `raidctx/` | `derive.go`, `providers.go`, `contribution.go` |
| `api.go` | `Optimize(ctx, req, progress) *proto.OptimizerResult`, `RunAsync` |

**Core seams:**
- `sim/core/database.go`:
  - `dbMu sync.RWMutex` and `AddToDatabase`, because `NewCharacter` adds to the global item maps on every
    UI request.
  - Read locks in `NewItem`.
  - An exported `EligibleSlotsForItem`.
- `sim/core/sheet.go`: `ComputePlayerSheet(raid, enc, raidIndex) PlayerSheet{FinalStats, MeleeCritTakenChance}`,
  built on `enemyCritChance`.

**Protos:**
- A new `proto/optimizer.proto`, importing only `common.proto`. The existing protoc lines pick it up
  through api.proto's import. It holds:
  - `OptimizerSettings`, `OptimizerEffort`, `SlotPool`, `ItemEnchantOptions`, `LimitGroup`,
    `MetaGemCondition`, `StatMinimum`
  - `CandidatePool` (with its `SimDatabase`)
  - `OptimizerLoadoutResult`, `OptimizerSlotAlternative`, `OptimizerProgress`
  - `OptimizerResult` (with the sim commit and catalog date), `OptimizerBatchExport`
  - `ServerCatalog`, `CatalogItem`, `CatalogSource`
- `api.proto` gets `OptimizeGearRequest`, plus `ProgressMetrics.final_optimize_result = 11` and
  `optimizer_progress = 12`.
- No edits to `Encounter`, `Target`, `UIItem` or `SimItem`.

### BIS-eval (wave B, done)

**Owns:** `evaluator.go`, `objective.go`, `response.go`, `residuals.go`, plus `BenchmarkOptimizerEval`
(Fury P1, Arcane P3), which pins the per-iteration cost and the effort budgets.

**Tests:**
- Sharded runs match unsharded within 1 se.
- The cloned request is unchanged after a run (`GetRaidBuffs` writes in place).
- Recover and cancel work.

ArP breakpoints differ per spell (Blood Gorged adds per-spell ArP), so take the ArP curve from sims, not
the sheet's cap.

**Interface for BIS-search:**
- `Evaluator.Evaluate(ctx, []Point, iterations)`. A `Point` is a `Loadout` plus bonus-stat `Offset`s,
  which only curves and normalizers set. `Delta(a, b, metric)` pairs over the iterations both ran.
- `SimEvaluator` runs 250-iteration shards, shard k at `RandomSeed + 250k` (Quick sims each as five
  50-iteration spans, with the same results), and extends a cached point
  by its missing shards. It keeps per-iteration samples, which pairing needs, only for the metrics it's
  given (`WeightedMetrics`). One failed sim fails the whole call with a `*SimError`, never cached; a
  cancel keeps finished shards.
- `NewObjective` measures each wₘ at ±100 of the spec's EP reference stat (±1000 Armor). A metric whose
  wₘ doesn't clear 2 se is left out with a warning. All-zero weights mean DPS; raid mode is an error.
- `MeasureResponse` fits, per stat, two lines through the seed meeting at a breakpoint, over the
  `ResponseRange` the pool reaches. A family is one sim stat: hit rating feeds the MeleeHit and SpellHit
  curves.
- `NeedsSim(ItemChoice)`: an effect on the item, a gem or the enchant, or weapon damage. Set bonuses need
  macros. `hardCodedItemIDs` holds 74 ids the sim checks directly, 62 without a registered effect; a
  source-scan test catches new checks.
- `MeasureResiduals`: paired sim Δ minus the curves' Δ over gear stats, counted from the seed.
- `EffortBudget` ([measured](bis-optimizer.INVESTIGATION.md#performance)), evaluations × iterations:
  Quick 250 × 500, Normal 500 × 4000, Thorough 1000 × 10000.

### BIS-raidctx (wave C, done)

**Owns:** `raidctx/derive.go`, `providers.go`, `testdata/` (the 25-player fixture and its golden).

`Derive`:
- empties the target's slot in a copy of the raid, calls `core.NewRaid` with
  `core.NewServerSettings(base.Encounter.GetServerSettings())`, then `GetRaidBuffs(clone)` and the party's
  `GetPartyBuffs(clone)`
- builds a debuff and replenishment table keyed by class, spec and talents, mirroring `RAID_STATS_OPTIONS`
  (`ui/raid/raid_stats.ts`)
- adds targeted buffs from spec-option `UnitReference`s
- maps Demonic Pact to `demonic_pact_sp`
- remaps the tank index to the target

**Tests:** synthetic rosters, and a golden of derived flags from a committed 25-player fixture. Rewrite the
golden with `go test --tags=with_db ./sim/optimizer/raidctx -run TestDeriveFixtureGolden -update`.

**Interface for BIS-search and BIS-raid-contrib:**
- `Derive(base, targetIndex) (*proto.RaidSimRequest, error)` returns the target alone at `TargetIndex` (0),
  so sim it there. It never changes `base`; a core panic comes back as an error. `raidctx` takes core and
  proto types and never imports `sim/optimizer`.
- Only active parties give, and never the target. Its `IndividualBuffs` (the raid UI's blessings) carry over,
  and derived buffs add to them.
- References to the target, in its settings (APL included) and `Raid.tanks`, move to index 0; references to
  other players are cleared. Tank entries keep their positions, so `tank_index` still lines up. Target
  dummies are dropped: a lone mage uses its Focus Magic uptime option.
- Table rows follow the UI's conditions even where the sim's differ: every warlock gives both curses, every
  warrior Sunder Armor, Thunder Clap and Demoralizing Shout.
  - Bloodlust and the shouts are rows: the sim only casts them.
  - Renewed Hope and Divine Guardian count raid-wide.
  - Vigilance and Pain Suppression aren't derived: no spec option names their target.
  - `TestProvidersMirrorRaidStats` fails on a `raid_stats.ts` provider nobody sorted.
- `DemonicPactSP(spellPower, points)` is the aura's formula, round(2% × points × spell power). `Derive`
  feeds it the sheet spell power of each warlock with a pet, from `core.NewEnvironment` on the full raid.
  That runs low: it misses combat-only spell power (Demonic Knowledge, the Life Tap glyph, procs) and the
  aura's ratchet. The fixture's demo warlock gets 288, against 415 averaged over a 50-iteration raid sim.
  BIS-e2e-perf's calibration should show it.

### BIS-search (wave D, done)

**Owns:** `surrogate.go`, `search.go`, `verify.go`, `neighborhood.go`, and the real `Optimize`, including
raidctx.

Build the evaluator with `NewSimEvaluator(r, WeightedMetrics(r.Settings)...)`: only the metrics it's given
get paired samples. The fake `Evaluator` fails a whole call on one bad point, like the real one.

Call `raidctx.Derive` only for the raid batch, after `PrepareRequest`, and sim its result at
`raidctx.TargetIndex`. The gem DP keeps one flag per distinct unique gem (2^u states, 81 unique gems in the
catalog). Whole-loadout regems aren't slow (`Pool.Gem` is under 10% of the search's CPU at 4.6k
candidates); if they get slow, count per kind (same colors, category and uniqueness): the top m of a kind
always dominate, so it stays exact.

**As built** (`run.execute`; stages Setup, Objective, Stat curves, Effects, Search, Verify, Alternatives):
- Curves: the surrogate joins each response curve's knots with straight lines, not its two-line fit,
  which misprices Ret P4's ArP and agility up to 2×. `addKnots` adds knots 1/4 and 1/16 of the way to each
  end of a stat's range (Quick: 1/8; cap-prone: 1/16, none at Quick). Residuals price against these curves
  (`run.measureResiduals`), not `MeasureResiduals`.
- Effects: set bonuses sim against a stat-matched twin (the base plus the pieces' stats as bonus stats),
  so they carry no curve error. The top 4/6/8 ring and trinket effects (by effort) get pair sims:
  Darkmoon Card: Greatness variants share one proc.
- Budget: effect screening takes up to 35% of the run and refinement 10%, capped to leave 35% for
  verification (20%), its race (10%) and the neighborhood.
- Search: each start (seed, greedy, warm starts, priced sets) gets 3/5/8 annealing runs of 10k/20k/40k
  moves on parallel forks, each polished, then the best with each slot's top 3 runners-up forced in. It
  keeps the top 20 by `setKey`: swapped rings or trinkets are one set.
- Pick: a seed that breaks a rule (a floor, the pool) loses to the best legal verified loadout, whatever
  it scores. `result` reruns the acceptance test on the final sims and lists the pick first in `top`.
- Racial search and crit immunity are real from BIS-tanks-racials (F) on. BIS-raid-contrib plugs its evaluator
  into `optimize(ctx, asked, simmed, eval, …)`.

**Tests:**
- A known-answer brute force with a fake `Evaluator`, and a floor the seed misses.
- A real Fury P1 trinket × ring brute force: the pick must be within 2 se. Skipped under `-race`.
- A slow suite (tag `optimizer_slow`: Fury P1, Combat Rogue P3, Fire Mage P3, Ret P4). Paired,
  J_opt ≥ J_preset − 2 se. It logs one `slow: spec=… phase=… effort=…` line per case; `-decisions` logs
  each stage's choices.
- CLI smoke: `wowsimcli optimize` on `sim/optimizer/testdata/search/fury_p1.json` (1.1k candidates),
  which `TestSearchTestdata -update` rewrites.

**The tab:**

- Results: Δ ± se, alternatives, the sheet with caps; only the result fields that are set.
- Actions: Equip (gear and racial traits), Save (via `GearTab.savedGearManager`, so it shows at once),
  export JSON. "Export request" downloads a request that `TestReplayFixtures` takes as a fixture.

Checked by hand on its dev port.

The web server answers 409 while another run is busy (naming its id for `/cancelAsync`) and cancels a
run nobody has polled for 2 minutes, keeping its result: show both, and let the user retry.

**Interface for BIS-batch-ui:** `buildOptimizeRequest` takes the raid request, target index, tab settings,
`CatalogIndex`, the gear picker's filters, the Player's `getItems`/`getEnchants` and the `Database`. It returns
the request and `seedChanges`, one line per trim.
- Items per slot: `getItems`, `filterItemsByFilters`, then the catalog: a source counts when its tier is

  and the row's professions, and excludes. Locked slots get no `SlotPool`.
- Gems follow the catalog's tier, faction and PvP rules, not source kinds. Enchants follow

  later gear.
- `plainBossTarget` copies `Encounter.defaultTargetProto()`, since `encounter.ts` won't load under Node.

### BIS-tanks-racials (wave F, done)

**Owns:** `sim/core/sheet.go`, `sim/optimizer/{racial,critimmunity}.go`, the tank slider, the per-phase
tank encounter, the tab controls.

It also adds the weapon enchant 3851 Titanguard to `tools/database/enchant_overrides.go`, which the sim's
hand-written list never had, so a tank imports 50 Stamina light. That's item data, so `db.json`
regenerates at integration, and no golden wears it.

**As built:**
- `core.ComputePlayerSheet(raid, encounter, raidIndex)` returns `PlayerSheet{FinalStats,
  MeleeCritTakenChance, MeleeAttacker}`, the crit chance from the boss's own auto attack
  (`enemyCritChance`). `MeleeAttacker` is false when nothing swings, so an unknown chance never reads as
  immune. `optimizer.playerSheet` wraps it and replaces `Pool.finalStats` wherever a result is built,
  which is what fills `melee_crit_taken_chance`.
- Crit immunity is a Defense floor. `requiredDefense` bisects bonus Defense from -(seed Defense) to
  +2000 rating for D*, the sheet Defense where the boss's crit reaches 0; `execute` appends it to

  warns when it is still critable.
- The slider is `TankWeights(survival)`: DTPS and TMI take half the survival share each, TPS the rest

- Stage "Racial screen", after Objective: `screenRacials` scores the seed under all 10 races at
  screening iterations and keeps the top 3 plus anything within 2 se of the 3rd, capped at 6: several
  races are worth nothing to a spec and would otherwise tie exactly. Each finalist gets its own gear search
  (`searchRaces`, see BIS-caps-race below), and verification sims them paired and picks the race. `Top`
  keys on gear *and* traits.
- `run.pinHealingModel` reruns `Character.GetPresimOptions` once at Setup, 100 iterations at seed 1
  with healing off and then 1.5x DTPS, and writes the Hps into `Base`. Without it, a healing model with
  Hps 0 and a cadence (or an encounter ending at a health threshold) makes every shard rerun core's
  presim: about 40% more sims, and each point's healing then follows its own damage taken, which skews
  the DTPS, TMI and death-chance deltas. BIS-tank-boss keeps it pinned.
- Tab: the survival/threat slider, a crit-immunity checkbox, the 6-metric editor under Advanced, and a
  racial-traits select. Searching is now the default for every spec. `individual_sim_ui.ts` registers
  the tab for tanks too.
- `pool_builder.ts` `TANK_BOSSES` (rebuilt by BIS-tank-boss and BIS-TANK-MAGIC) maps each phase to a preset
  path and `raid_difficulty`: Patchwerk 25,
  Algalon 25, Anub'arak 25H, Lich King 25H for P4 and P5 (unset reads as 25 normal). It forces the

### BIS-caps (wave L, done)

The user's call (2026-09-29): picks may sit under a hit or expertise cap, which beats capping in the sim's
fight from behind, but a raid can't always attack from behind. In the phase 2 batch a DPS death knight had 112
expertise rating of the 202 the dodge cap needs, and a DPS warrior 206 of 212. So DPS specs get hard floors,
added the way `critImmunityFloor` adds Defense (one floor per stat, raised rather than joined by a second):

- Melee: hit rating to the special-attack miss cap (not the dual-wield white cap), and expertise to the dodge
  cap. Hunters: ranged hit, no expertise. Casters: spell hit to the spell miss cap.
- Each cap is exact, the rating that brings that chance against the run's target to 0 on the sim's own combat
  table (`MagicSpellHitResult`: 17% against a boss on the live config), after talents, racials and the run's
  buffs and debuffs. Bisect it like `requiredDefense`, reading the chances off `core.ComputePlayerSheet`. The
  caps a result reports come from response-curve breakpoints, which can be a few rating off.
- Tanks get none of these floors.
- Casters and hunters also get a haste ceiling (the user's calls, the same day). Casters: the haste rating at
  which a hasted global cooldown reaches its 1 s floor (`Spell::TriggerGlobalCooldown`'s `MIN_GCD`), about 1,270
  with Wrath of Air and a 3% haste aura. Hunters, whose GCD haste doesn't touch: the rating at which Steady
  Shot casts in 1 s, about 1,335 with a quiver, Serpent's Swiftness and a 3% aura. Both count talents and the
  run's lasting buffs, not Bloodlust or Heroism, other temporary cooldowns or procs (Improved Aspect of the
  Hawk's). Melee get none.
- A "Respect hit, expertise and haste caps" checkbox in the BiS tab and the BiS Batch tab, ticked by default,
  saved and restored with their other settings (settings saved before it restore ticked). Presets and the
  tooltip dataset use the default.
- A floor the pool can't reach doesn't fail the run: the pick gets as close as it can, and the result warns.
- Tests: a melee DPS whose unfloored pick sits under the expertise cap reaches it; a caster reaches the spell
  hit cap; the floor follows the buffs (Heroic Presence lowers the hit floor); a caster and a hunter whose
  unconstrained picks pass their haste ceiling stop at it; a tank run gets none (a melee-hit floor since

#### As built (review fix: the search slowdown)

Review found the caps' default-on floor routed nearly every DPS search through `search.go`'s slow
path: `configure()` fell back from `configureFast` (an O(1) delta per enchant/reforge trial) to
`tryAll`, a full `surrogate.score` rescore over every curved stat, whenever `len(s.floors) > 0` —
previously only the rare tank crit-immunity case. Isolated A/B (same request, caps on vs. off):
fury_p1 phase 1 Quick 2.1x slower, a Marksmanship hunter 8.7x, and a hunter over the full phase 1-5
pool didn't finish inside Go's 600 s test timeout at all.

Floors and ceilings are already linear in gear stats (`floorShortfall`, `ceilingOverage`), so their
trial-to-trial cost can be an O(1) delta too, the same way `statsGain` already priced the response
curves' share of a trial. `surrogate.capGain` (`sim/optimizer/surrogate.go`) adds that delta;
`configure` now always calls `configureFast`, and the `tryAll` full-rescore path is gone.

Profiling the fix (`go test -cpuprofile`, hunter caps-on) found two more costs worth cutting, both
from `floorTerm`/`ceilingTerm` carrying their gradient as a `[stats.Len]float64` (~470 B): ranging
`for _, f := range s.floors` copied the whole struct per floor per call, and a cap's gradient is
almost always nonzero in one stat (its own rating), so scanning all of `stats.Len` for it was mostly
wasted work. Both `floorTerm` and `ceilingTerm` now carry a `sparse []gradEntry` (built once in
`setFloorsAndCeilings`) alongside the dense `gradient`; every hot read (`capGain`, `floorShortfall`,
`ceilingOverage`, `slopes`, `regime`, `slopeBox`) uses `sparse` and indexes `s.floors`/`s.ceilings` by
pointer instead of copying. A literal `floorTerm`/`ceilingTerm` built outside `setFloorsAndCeilings`
(`TestSlopeBoxFloors`, `TestCeilingPenalizesOverage`) must set `sparse` too, via `sparseGradient`.

Same isolated A/B after the fix: fury_p1 1.25x, the Marksmanship hunter 1.88x, and the phase 1-5 pool
that didn't finish before now takes about 30 s. The fix is exact, not an approximation swap —
`configureFast` already matched a full rescore bit-for-bit with no floors, and `capGain` is the same
math computed incrementally — so it left `optimizer_slow`'s numbers unchanged, run to run, and all 37
goldens too. The remaining 1.25x-1.88x is the caps' own,
proportionate share of the existing per-candidate cost (`capGain` fell from the top-2 CPU consumer to
about 3% of it), not a runaway path; `acbis`'s driver and `presetgen` call the same `Optimize`, so
they see the same fix, though this item didn't re-time them directly.

Also closed from the review: `TestMeleeCapFloorsReachTheCap` and `TestCasterCapFloorReachesTheCap` now
fail if the unconstrained (caps-off) pick no longer sits under its floor, so a future pool or
item-data change that makes a floor a no-op can't pass silently. A hunter equivalent of
`TestCasterHasteCeilingStopsTheSearch` doesn't exist for a different reason, not an oversight:
Steady Shot's ceiling needs about 100% ranged haste, and a phase 5 realistic pool's DPS-optimal
unconstrained pick never comes close (199 rating against a ~2258 ceiling) — unlike a caster, where
haste is strong enough that the unconstrained search overshoots on its own. `TestHunterHasteCeilingNeverBindsARealisticPool`
covers the hunter ceiling end to end instead (a real search, caps on, over that pool, never exceeds
it); `TestHunterCapsAreExact` already covers the ceiling's own bisection, and
`TestCeilingPenalizesOverage` the surrogate term, both spec-agnostically.

### BIS-TANK-MAGIC (wave Z8, done)

The raid pass gears both tanks for avoidance and no hit. At phase 3 (the 8 Oct pass, sim `7f1b9aede`) Bulwark
(prot paladin) got 16 of 23 gems agility, one stamina gem (the meta) and 0 hit rating; Felesta (tank DK) got 13
dodge gems, 9 defense, 1039 defense rating against a floor of 566, and hit reforged off two pieces. The search
works as designed; the model it searches against is wrong:

1. **No magic, no abilities.** `genericBossTargetAt` (`pool_builder.ts`) builds one attacker that only swings. It
   sets no `id`, and an AI attaches only to a registered preset's id (`sim/core/target.go`).
2. **Against pure physical, mitigation compounds.** Armor takes 62% (Felesta) and 69% (Bulwark), on top of
   avoidance and of defense far past the floor: Felesta takes 1033 DTPS from a boss set for about 41,600 a second.
3. **Stamina can't reach the score.** DTPS is the damage that landed and TPS ignores stamina; only TMI's
   `amount / MaxHealth()` sees it. Chance of death, the metric stamina moves most, weighs 0 (`TankWeights`,
   `tankMetricWeights`).
4. **The tank can't die.** `healingModelForBoss` pins HPS to 0.175 × the boss's unmitigated damage with no cadence
   variation: about 4,620 HPS against Felesta's 1,033 DTPS, death chance 0.00025. A set HPS also skips the presim
   that derives 1.5 × the tank's own DTPS (`sim/core/health.go`, `pinHealingModel`).

**Taunts** (wrong, as the cross-review below found: the four are `DmgClass MAGIC` and roll spell hit; the sim
has no taunt spell, and enemies never retarget by threat): Taunt,
Growl, Dark Command and Hand of Reckoning are `DmgClass MELEE` without `SPELL_ATTR3_ALWAYS_HIT` or
`SPELL_ATTR7_NO_ATTACK_MISS`, so they take `MeleeSpellHitResult`: 8% miss against a level 83 boss, removed 1:1 by
melee hit rating (263, or 164 with 3/3 Precision); spell hit does nothing. They also roll dodge 6.45% and, from
the front, parry 14.0%, which only expertise lowers. Bot tanks re-taunt until they hold aggro.

**Decided by the user:** fix the fight model, no hand-set health or stamina floor; a tank melee-hit floor at the
taunt cap, hit only; afterwards the full 21-raider pass, on the user's call
([driver README](../../tools/database/acbis/driver/README.md)) (2026-10-11). Phase 1's boss is Kel'Thuzad 25, not
Patchwerk, who has next to no mechanics, and the raid interrupts half of his single-target Frostbolts
(2026-10-12).

**Step 0, the numbers.** For each `TANK_BOSSES` boss at its difficulty, every ability that damages the current
tank and that armor and avoidance can't remove: spell id, school, damage, cadence. Sources: the boss script
under [ac] `src/server/scripts/`, Spell.dbc and SpellDifficulty.dbc, `creature_template`. A first table with its
evidence is in `G:\DevStuff\GitHub\.wave-loop\bis-tank-magic\step0.md` (research run at the wave's start): check
it against the source, then carry it into the As built. Server data only: a small magic share is modelled small,
and a boss with none stays melee-only.
